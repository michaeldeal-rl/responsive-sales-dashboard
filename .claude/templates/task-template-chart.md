# Chart Task Template

## Goal
Describe the chart change requested.

## Scope
- Chart logic: `js/scripts.js`
- Optional styling: `css/styles.css`

## Requirements
- Keep the data realistic and consistent.
- Preserve the chosen chart theme and legend behavior.
- Avoid breaking chart rendering or responsiveness.

## Validation
- Open the page with Live Server.
- Confirm the chart loads and the chart area remains visually aligned.
